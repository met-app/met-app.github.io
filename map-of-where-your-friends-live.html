How to make a map of where all your friends live

Updated November 3, 2026

If you've lived abroad, studied on an exchange or traveled a lot, your friends are probably spread across many countries. A map of where they all live is surprisingly useful: it shows you who to visit when you travel, who's nearby when you move, and just how far your friendships reach.

Option 1: A spreadsheet

The simplest start is a sheet with a column for name, city and country. It's free and flexible, but it doesn't show you anything visually, and it quickly falls out of date because it's a pain to edit on your phone.

Option 2: A custom map tool

General-purpose map tools let you drop pins on a custom map. They work, but they're built for places, not people. There's nowhere natural to keep how you met, a birthday or a WhatsApp number, and adding a friend on your phone is fiddly.

Option 3: An app made for it

MET is an app built specifically to map your friends. Each friend is a pin in the city they live in, with their photo on the pin. Every country where you have friends is shaded, and MET tells you how many friends you have in how many countries.

Because it's made for people, each friend also has a profile with where you met, their birthday, nationality, notes and contacts. You can search everyone, browse them by country, and add new friends by scanning their QR code.

What to save for each friend

  • The city they live in, not just the country. It makes the map far more useful when you travel.
  • How you met, so you remember the story behind each pin.
  • How to reach them, ideally the app you'd actually message them on.
  • Their birthday, a good reason to get back in touch once a year.

Keep it up to date

People move. Whenever a friend mentions they've moved city, update their pin. If you set a habit of checking your map before each trip, keeping it current becomes part of planning, not a chore.

Privacy matters

A map of your friends is personal information about other people. MET stores everything on your phone, with no account and no tracking, and never uses anyone's live location. You choose where each friend lives yourself.
