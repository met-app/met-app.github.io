MET features

A map of everyone you've met, and the details you don't want to forget.

MET is a free app for iPhone and Android that shows where in the world your friends live. You pin each friend in the city they live in, and MET keeps how you met, their birthday, nationality and contact details with them.

A map of your friends

  • Every friend on one world map, pinned in the city they live in, with their photo as the pin.
  • Countries light up when you have a friend there, and MET counts how many friends you have in how many countries.
  • Friends in the same city share one pin with a count, so busy cities stay readable.
  • Find friend: search by name, city, how you met or your notes, and the map flies to them.
  • Light and dark maps that follow your phone's appearance, or a setting of your choice.

Profiles that remember how you met

  • A photo from your camera or photo library.
  • Where you met, for example "hostel in Lisbon, summer 2024".
  • Birthday, with or without the year. MET shows their age when the year is known.
  • Nationality and where they live. Search for any city or tap the map, and MET works out the country, even offline.
  • Free-form notes for anything else worth remembering.

Stay in touch in one tap

Save a friend's phone number, email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Snapchat and Facebook. Tapping one opens their profile straight in that app, or starts a call or email.

Add friends with a QR code

Create your own profile and show it as a QR code. A friend scans it, in MET or with their regular camera, and gets your name, birthday, city and contacts filled in. They only add where you met before saving.

Your friends, sorted by country

The Friends list groups everyone by the country they live in, with the biggest countries first, and you can search all of it.

Private by design

MET has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your friends' details and photos are stored on your phone. MET never uses your location or theirs: you choose where each friend lives yourself.

MET Premium

  • Sync with iCloud on iPhone, or Google Drive on Android, to keep your friends on all your devices.
  • Backup and restore: save everything, including photos, to one file and restore it on any phone.
  • No more paywalls.

Premium is available as a weekly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.
