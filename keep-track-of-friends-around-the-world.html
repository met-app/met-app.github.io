How to keep track of friends who live all over the world

Updated November 3, 2026

Friendships across borders are some of the best you'll have, and some of the easiest to lose. Different countries, time zones and messaging apps make it hard to keep track of who lives where and when you last spoke. A little structure goes a long way.

Organize friends by where they live

Your phone's contacts app sorts people alphabetically, which is no help when you're wondering who you know in Tokyo. Organizing friends by city and country makes them easy to find when it matters: when you travel, move or have a layover.

MET does this on a world map. Every friend is a pin in their city, and the Friends list groups everyone by country, with a search across names, places and how you met.

Remember the context

With friends from many places, details blur together. Was it Sofia from the Rome hostel or the one from the language course? Saving a line about how you met each person keeps the stories straight, and makes it easier to pick up where you left off.

Keep their contacts in one place

International friends often live on different apps: WhatsApp for one, Instagram for another, email for someone else. Keep every friend's preferred contact together so getting in touch is one tap, not a search through five apps.

Use birthdays as reminders

A birthday message is the easiest excuse to reconnect. Save each friend's birthday, even without the year, and use it as a yearly check-in.

Plan trips around people

Before booking a trip, look at your map. A visit to a friend can turn an ordinary trip into a memorable one, and a quick "I'm passing through your city" message keeps friendships alive for years.

Back it up

A list you've built over years of travel is worth protecting. MET Premium syncs your friends with iCloud or Google Drive and can save a backup file you keep anywhere.
