MET FAQ

Answers to common questions about MET.

What is MET?

MET is an app for iPhone and Android that shows where in the world your friends live. You pin each friend in their city on a world map and save how you met, their birthday, nationality, contact details and notes.

Is MET free?

Yes, MET is free to download and use. MET Premium adds iCloud or Google Drive sync and backup and restore, and is available as a weekly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Does MET track my location or my friends' locations?

No. MET never uses GPS or live location. You choose where each friend lives yourself, by searching for a city or tapping the map. It is a map of where your friends are based, not a live location tracker.

Do my friends need MET?

No. You can add anyone by hand. Your friends only need MET if they want to share their own profile with you as a QR code.

How do I add a friend with a QR code?

Your friend opens My profile in MET to show their QR code. In your app, tap Add friend, then Scan QR code. Their name, birthday, city and contacts are filled in, and you add where you met before saving. Scanning the code with the regular camera works too.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. MET has no account and no server that stores your friends. If you turn on Premium sync, your data is also copied to your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android), where only you can access it.

Can I back up my friends?

Yes, with MET Premium. Back up saves all friends and photos to a single file that you can keep on your phone or share to email or cloud storage, and Restore brings them back on any phone.

Does MET work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Sync works between devices on the same platform: iCloud between Apple devices, Google Drive between Android devices. To move friends between an iPhone and an Android phone, use Backup and Restore.

Can I see which countries I have friends in?

Yes. Every country where you have a friend is shaded on the map, and MET shows how many friends you have in how many countries. The Friends list also groups everyone by country.

Does MET work offline?

Your friends, profiles and contacts are on your phone and work offline. Loading map images and searching for a place need an internet connection.

Can I use MET on an iPad?

MET is made for iPhone, and the iPhone app also runs on iPad.

How do I cancel MET Premium?

The weekly subscription is managed by Apple or Google. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment with nothing to cancel.
